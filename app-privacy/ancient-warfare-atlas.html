Privacy Policy — Ancient Warfare Atlas

Last updated: 24 October 2026

App: Ancient Warfare Atlas
Developer: PRANSBO Tech Corp

1. Overview

Ancient Warfare Atlas is an offline historical reference application that provides historical articles and educational content. The app does not require users to create an account.

2. Information Collection and Sharing

Ancient Warfare Atlas is designed not to collect or transmit personal information to the developer. In this release, the app does not include advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or remote user-tracking features. Historical articles are bundled with the application and are available offline.

3. Information Stored on Your Device

The app may store article bookmarks in Android's app-private local storage so users can return to saved articles. These bookmarks are not transmitted by the app. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the application may remove them.

4. Permissions

The app's historical reading features do not require access to contacts, location, camera, microphone, or photos. If future versions introduce features that change the permissions requested or the way information is handled, this policy will be updated as appropriate.

5. External Links

The app may provide links to external websites or other applications. When users follow an external link, the destination service may process information according to its own privacy policy and practices. The developer does not control external services.

6. Children

Ancient Warfare Atlas is a historical reference application. The developer does not intend to use the app to collect personal information from children. The app's target audience and applicable requirements are addressed through the relevant distribution platform's settings and policies.

7. Data Security and Retention

Because the app is designed not to transmit personal information to the developer, the developer does not maintain a server-side personal-data record of app usage through the app. Bookmarks are stored locally and may remain on the device until deleted, app data is cleared, or the app is uninstalled.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This policy may be updated if the app's features or information handling practices change. The current version will be published at this URL.
